## Conversion strategy

The site answers four high-intent roofing questions in order: What is wrong? Can someone help now? What are my options? Why should I trust this company? The homepage includes a priority storm/leak request form, while the inspection page captures full project details.

## Included

- Three responsive pages
- Priority storm and roof-leak request form
- Detailed roof-inspection and estimate form
- Roof replacement, repair, storm, gutter, financing, and claim-support content
- Pittsburgh-area local SEO scaffold
- Open Graph, sitemap, canonical tags, and RoofingContractor schema
- Mobile navigation and accessible form labels
- Safety language for structural, electrical, and tree-impact hazards

## Demo protections

- Forms validate locally but transmit and store nothing.
- Demo phone uses a reserved 555 number.
- Reviews are visibly labeled as samples.
- The site is `noindex, nofollow`, and `robots.txt` disallows crawling.
- No unverified claims about licensing, insurance, certifications, warranties, response time, or insurance coverage are published.

## Customer launch checklist

- Replace the brand, colors, copy, services, photos, and contact information.
- Verify company address, counties served, license/insurance language, certifications, warranties, and financing disclosures.
- Replace sample reviews with approved customer reviews and obtain image permissions.
- Connect forms to approved email/CRM/dispatch destination and add spam protection, consent, privacy, and retention rules.
- Update canonical domain, sitemap, structured data, and social preview.
- Remove demo protections only after approval.
