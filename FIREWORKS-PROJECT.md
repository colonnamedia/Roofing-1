## Conversion strategy

The site answers high-intent roofing questions in order: What is wrong? What are my options? Where does the company work? What project evidence is available? The homepage includes a storm/leak intake preview, while the inspection page captures full project details. No emergency availability or response time is promised.

## Demo protections

- Forms validate locally but transmit and store nothing.
- Demo phone uses a reserved 555 number.
- Reviews are visibly labeled as samples.
- The site is `noindex, nofollow`, and `robots.txt` disallows crawling.
- No unverified claims about licensing, insurance, certifications, warranties, response time, or insurance coverage are published.
- Generated sample copy, gallery photos, location coverage and testimonials require client verification or replacement.

## Customer launch checklist

- Replace the brand, colors, copy, services, photos, and contact information.
- Verify company address, counties served, license/insurance language, certifications, warranties, and financing disclosures.
- Replace sample reviews with approved customer reviews and obtain image permissions.
- Connect forms to approved email/CRM/dispatch destination and add spam protection, consent, privacy, and retention rules.
- Update canonical domain, sitemap, structured data, and social preview.
- Remove demo protections only after approval.

## Client inputs and future variations

Required: business identity, actual service territory, verified phone and hours, roof specialties, project photos and permissions, warranty/certification evidence, real testimonials, and chosen lead destination. Future hero directions: close-up repair detail, storm inspection, and completed home exterior. Real before-and-after comparisons require matching photos of the same property.
