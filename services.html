Roofing services

The right solution starts with the right diagnosis.

We look at the entire roof system—not just the obvious symptom—then explain the options that make sense for your home, timeline, and budget.

01

Roof Replacement

A complete, coordinated roofing system designed for the architecture of your home and the demands of Western Pennsylvania weather.

  • Architectural asphalt shingles
  • Underlayment, ice and water protection
  • Flashing and ventilation review
  • Decking inspection and documented change orders
  • Property protection and magnetic cleanup
02

Roof Repair

Targeted repairs that address the source of the problem, with photos and a plain-language explanation of what was corrected.

  • Leak tracing and repair
  • Missing, lifted, or damaged shingles
  • Pipe boots, flashing, and penetrations
  • Chimney and wall transition repairs
  • Emergency weatherproofing options
03

Storm Damage

A careful inspection after wind, hail, falling branches, or severe weather—with findings you can understand and keep.

  • Photo-documented roof assessment
  • Wind, hail, and impact indicators
  • Temporary mitigation recommendations
  • Scope review and project explanation
  • Homeowner-controlled insurance process
04

Gutters & Drainage

Roof-edge and water-management improvements that help protect siding, landscaping, basements, and foundations.

  • Seamless gutter installation
  • Downspout sizing and placement
  • Gutter guards
  • Fascia and soffit evaluation
  • Drainage-path recommendations

Repair or replace?

We’ll show you the difference.

Not every roof problem means replacement. We consider age, material condition, repair history, ventilation, active leaks, storm exposure, and how long you expect to own the home.

✓
Repair

Best when damage is contained and the surrounding roof still has useful life.

✓
Monitor

Appropriate when a roof is aging but stable and no immediate work is justified.

✓
Replace

Recommended when condition, repeat failures, or system-wide wear make repairs poor value.
